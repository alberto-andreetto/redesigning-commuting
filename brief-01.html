Redesigning the commute.

The challenge.

Design a feature that improves a specific moment of the daily commute experience within an existing mobility or transport service — e.g., train, bus, ride-sharing, bike-sharing.

Why it matters.

Commuting is one of the most repetitive, high-friction parts of daily life. Small improvements can compound into significant quality-of-life gains. AI offers new opportunities to reduce uncertainty, personalize information, and make transit feel more predictable.

Key pain points.

  • Delays and unexpected changes
  • Overcrowding and discomfort
  • Uncertainty about arrival times
  • Boredom during waiting or transit time
  • Complicated transfers and last-mile connections
  • Information scattered across apps

Design focus.

Pick one specific moment or problem in the commute journey:

  • Waiting at the station
  • Planning a trip
  • Recovering from a delay
  • Choosing between transport modes
  • The first/last mile

Success criteria.

  • The feature integrates naturally into an existing service
  • It demonstrably reduces friction or anxiety
  • It uses AI in a meaningful way — not as a gimmick
  • It can be prototyped and tested within the course

Example directions.

  • An AI assistant that predicts and explains delays in plain language
  • A "calm commute" mode that curates content based on estimated wait time
  • A community-powered delay feed combined with official data
  • A personalized multi-modal trip optimizer

Deliverables.

Research and insights · Concept and feature map · User flows and wireframes · High-fidelity screens · Working prototype or coded demo · Case study presentation.

Choosing this brief.

If this is your brief, write exactly three sentences — who the user is, what the core problem is, and why this problem interests you — and commit them as brief.md in your repository.
